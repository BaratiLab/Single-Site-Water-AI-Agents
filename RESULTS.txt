WHAT CHANGED:
Joint radial-shell updates, independent positive angular strength, stabilized coordination terms, sparse undercoordination corrections and broader second-shell density descriptors were tested. Original numerical experimental gOO at295.15K replaces OPC3 as the primary structural reference. Native mW was simulated under matching protocols.
The actual upstream LLM-SR buffer was updated repeatedly between adaptive batches. Its agent-authored file sampler is a bounded, transparent workflow; numerical fitting and MD are external. No exhaustive optimum or external API search is claimed.

FAILED HYPOTHESES:
Born/fluctuation ranking failed its EOS check and was replaced. Large radial-coordinate warps caused severe pressure/structure failures; those models remain recorded. Early static counterterms often failed after relaxation. Incorrect-lookup repeated-parent runs are preserved but excluded from intended-family selection.

REMAINING GAPS:
A cohesion proxy is not coexistence vaporization enthalpy. Raw CG heat capacities omit molecular degrees of freedom. No intrinsic dipole/dielectric relaxation exists in this neutral position-only Hamiltonian. New-model diffusion, viscosity, surface tension, TMD, melting, boiling/vapor pressure and thermal conductivity require their own validation; old results do not transfer.
Transport calibration is conditional on acceptable equilibrium sampling and must fit diffusion/viscosity together. A thermostat cannot fix an equilibrium EOS error.

See SYMBOLIC_MODEL.txt, round6_engine/REPRODUCE.txt, METHOD_LIMITS.txt and the compact source/audit bundle. Raw MD trajectories remain in the workspace.
