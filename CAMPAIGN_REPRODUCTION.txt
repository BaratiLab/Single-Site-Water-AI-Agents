Model semantics and units
-------------------------
U=sum_pairs phi(r)+sum_i[F(n_i)+Uunder(n_i)+L(n_i)*A_i].
The radial phi is an interval cubic with its declared knots and coefficients.
Coordination n=sum_j(1-r²/rd²)^3 inside rd. Angular A is the exact unordered-pair
invariant sum_j<k w_ij*w_ik*(cos(theta)+1/3)², with optional positive polynomial
w=s³Q(s)², s=max(0,1-r²/rc²). General positive L=l0+l1delta+l2delta² is used only
when angular_polynomial is present; otherwise the previous square is retained.
F includes K1/K2 and optional h3/h4 powers in(n−n0). Analytic forces include
L'(n)*A, not merely L*gradient(A). The full six-component global virial is native.

Parameters use kJ/mol and Angstrom; the plugin divides energy/force by4.184 to
obey LAMMPS units real. Thermo energy is system kcal/mol, pressure atm, time fs.
Multiply energy by4.184 for system kJ/mol, pressure by1.01325 for bar, and divide
LAMMPS time by1000 for ps. Historical JSON time metadata in ps does not change
the integrator's real-unit timestep. Mass is18.01528g/mol numerically18.01528amu.

E_iso=K2*n0²−K1*n0−h3*n0³+h4*n0⁴+A3+A4, generally NOT zero. Include this
reference in cohesion diagnostics; it is not a molecular electronic energy.
The separate electric/caloric closures are not intrinsic fluctuations of this
neutral mechanical Hamiltonian.

Limits and safe execution
--------------------------
One species, serial orthogonal periodic cells only. atom_modify map array and
sort0 are required. Every box length must remain greater than twice the largest
radial/coordination/angular support throughout the run, not merely at startup.
The current engine checks the initial cell; monitor NPT cells separately. Do not
apply two integrators to the same atoms. After changing/removing fixes, perform
ordinary setup (e.g.run0 with default preyes) before pre-no continuation; stale
fix setup previously caused a corrected transport-runner crash.

MPI decomposition, triclinic geometry, restart serialization, per-atom energy,
per-atom stress, heat flux and production timestep scaling remain unqualified.
Short NVE/FD mechanics checks do not prove EOS, phase equilibria, transport,
production stability or universal thermodynamic stability. The quartic guard
removes an obvious unstable coordination-polynomial direction; it is not a
complete mathematical stability proof for arbitrary proposed coefficients.

These unexecuted examples are not evidence of completed MD. During an active
fitting/validation campaign, exclusive timing must wait until other MD is idle.
Benchmark scripts record actual state and stability rather than assuming
accuracy-equivalent water dynamics.
